# Interaction Study 3 · Crumple · Crushing

The statement sits on a plain ground. Two white-glove hands crush it into a low-poly ball.

- **Source artifact:** https://claude.ai/artifact/1iYeuN8Myc44zuVyMn9s2W
- **Live page:** https://klitsner.github.io/IXD-107-sequential-interaction-prototype/interaction-study-3-crumple/

## How to interact
- Hover over the statement: two open hands fade in at its sides.
- Press and hold: the hands close in and shake harder as the paper is crushed into a ball. Releasing early lets it partly spring back.
- ↺ (top right) resets the study.

## Files
- `index.html` — the complete artifact, copied unchanged from the published version. All CSS, JavaScript and SVG assets are inline.

## Setup and dependencies
None. There are no external libraries, fonts, images or network requests.

- **Run locally:** open `index.html` in a modern desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). Or serve the folder, for example `python3 -m http.server`, and visit `http://localhost:8000/interaction-study-3-crumple/`.
- **Input:** mouse or pen only. Touch input is ignored by design.
- **Sound:** uses the Web Audio API. Audio starts after your first click on the page.
